# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want ISynKGR to be a professional, respectful, and technically rigorous research community. Participants are expected to engage constructively regardless of background, experience level, employer, academic affiliation, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, evidence, code, and experimental methodology rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable review feedback and assume good intent while still requiring scientific rigor.
- Be transparent about limitations, conflicts of interest, reused material, and benchmark changes.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, licensing, and data-governance constraints.
- Credit prior work and contributors appropriately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, plagiarism, falsification of results, undisclosed manipulation of benchmark outcomes, or publication of confidential/security-sensitive information is not acceptable.

## Research integrity

Because ISynKGR is a benchmark repository, contributors must not intentionally hide configuration changes, selectively omit failed runs, alter ground truth without disclosure, or present non-comparable experiments as directly comparable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers rather than opening a public issue.
